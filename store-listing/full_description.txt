OfflinePlaya is a music player for the music you actually own. No streaming, no accounts, no ads, no cloud — just your files, on your device.

Point it at your music and it builds a clean library you can browse by artist, album, folder, or track, and organize into playlists.

PLAY TO YOUR PC (NEW)
Stream your phone's audio live, over your own WiFi, to a free companion app on your Windows, Mac, or Linux computer — and play it through your real speakers or hi-fi. Your phone stays in control; nothing is ever saved on the PC, and the stream never leaves your home network. Turn your home stereo into a jukebox, no cables and no Bluetooth.

FEATURES
• 100% offline — plays the audio files already on your phone (MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A and more)
• Built-in equalizer — hand-tuned presets, a manual 10-band graph, or Auto mode that picks a preset from each track's genre, plus a loudness preamp
• USB DAC support — bit-perfect output on Android 14+ for an untouched signal to your DAC, and clean playback through any other USB DAC
• Gapless playback, optional crossfade, and hardware-volume control
• Synced lyrics — reads embedded lyrics and .lrc files, or fetches them when missing
• Album art that just works — embedded covers, folder images, or an online lookup when needed
• Smart playlists & listening history — Recently played, Most played, Forgotten favorites and more, built from how you actually listen
• Write tags back into your files — embed album art and genre so other apps see them too
• Android Auto — browse and play your library in the car
• Material 3 design with a dark theme, optional Material You wallpaper colors, and theming drawn from your album art
• Available in 41 languages

PRIVACY
OfflinePlaya has no accounts and no tracking. It only reaches the internet to look up the album art and lyrics you ask for — your library never leaves your device, and Play to PC stays on your local network.

OfflinePlaya is for people who keep their own music collection and want a fast, private, good-looking player — not another streaming subscription.
