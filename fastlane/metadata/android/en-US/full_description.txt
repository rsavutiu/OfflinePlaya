OfflinePlaya is an offline music player for the music you already own. It plays the local audio files on your phone — MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WAV, Opus — with no streaming, no accounts, no ads and no cloud.

Point it at your music and it builds a clean library you can browse by artist, album, folder or track, and organize into playlists. Everything works in airplane mode.

WHY A LOCAL MUSIC PLAYER
If you have a collection — ripped CDs, bandcamp downloads, bootlegs, DJ sets, audiobooks, field recordings — a streaming app is the wrong tool. OfflinePlaya is built for files: fast library scanning, real folder browsing, and tags you can fix yourself.

PLAY TO YOUR PC
Stream your phone's audio live, over your own WiFi, to a free companion app on your Windows, Mac or Linux computer — and play it through your real speakers or hi-fi. Your phone stays in control; nothing is ever saved on the PC, and the stream never leaves your home network. Turn your home stereo into a jukebox, with no cables and no Bluetooth.

FEATURES
• 100% offline MP3 and FLAC player — plays the audio files already on your phone, including M4A, OGG, WAV and Opus
• 10-band equalizer — hand-tuned presets (Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, EDM, Hip-Hop, Bass Boost, Vocal Boost and more), a manual graph, or Auto mode that picks a preset from each track's genre, plus a loudness preamp
• USB DAC support — bit-perfect output on Android 14+ for an untouched signal to your DAC, and clean playback through any other USB DAC
• Gapless playback, optional crossfade, and hardware-volume control
• Synced lyrics — reads embedded lyrics and .lrc files, or fetches them when missing
• Album art that just works — embedded covers, folder images, or an online lookup when needed
• Smart playlists and listening history — Recently played, Most played, Forgotten favorites, Never played, built from how you actually listen
• Folder browsing and a tag editor that writes back into your files, so other apps see your fixes too
• Android Auto — browse and play your library in the car
• Material 3 design with a dark theme, optional Material You wallpaper colors, and theming drawn from your album art
• Available in 40+ languages

PRIVACY
OfflinePlaya has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It only reaches the internet to look up the album art and lyrics you ask for — your library never leaves your device, and Play to PC stays on your local network.

OfflinePlaya is for people who keep their own music collection and want a fast, private, good-looking offline player — not another streaming subscription.
